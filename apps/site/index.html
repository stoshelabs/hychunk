HyChunk: Hytale world map

HyChunk is a free, open source map for Hytale worlds. Open a server save or enter a seed and explore the terrain, structures, dungeons, portals and mob spawn areas in your browser. Chunks nobody visited are predicted from the world seed.

  • Reads real saves: regions, chunks, blocks, markers, players and plugin worlds
  • Predicts unexplored chunks from the seed, including trees, monuments, camps and the spawn room
  • Place finder with search, filters, a done list and CSV export
  • Mob spawn areas and biome highlighting
  • Shareable seed links, versioned per Hytale release
